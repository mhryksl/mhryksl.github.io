Privacy Policy — Spaceship Battle Fleet

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Applies to com.mhr.spaceshipbattlefleet (Spaceship Battle Fleet)

Spaceship Battle Fleet is an online space draft battle game published by Mahir Yüksel. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does not do with information.

Short version

  • You can play as a guest with no sign-up. Signing in with your Google account is optional: if you do, the game uses your Google account's name as your player name and keeps a copy of your progress online. We never ask for your phone number or location.
  • To offer matches against other players, online rivals, a leaderboard and moving your progress to another phone, the game connects to our game server (Google Firebase) with an anonymous account created for your device. It stores your player name, your trophies, statistics and deck. You can delete it in the game.
  • The game shows ads served by Google AdMob; most are optional reward videos. Google's ad software collects some device information, described below.
  • Optional purchases are made and paid through Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Reminders (notifications) are optional and are made by the game on your phone itself; nothing about them is sent anywhere.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally: cards, decks, levels, trophies, in-game currencies, crates, quests, season pass progress, special ammo, purchase flags (for example whether "No Ads" was bought), timers for in-game rewards, and your settings: sound and music volume, vibration, language, and whether you want notifications. It also keeps the session token for the game server. This stays in the app's private storage; if you use Android's backup, Android may include it in your own device backup.

Game server (Google Firebase)

When you are online, the game creates an anonymous account for your device with Firebase Authentication and stores game data in Cloud Firestore, both provided by Google LLC as part of Firebase. Linked to that random account ID it stores:

  • Public profile: your player name and the avatar you chose, trophies, wins and losses, and your current deck with card levels. Other players can see these on the leaderboard, and your deck may be used, shown with your player name, avatar and trophies, as an AI-controlled rival for players with similar trophies. A guest plays under a name the game makes up (for example "Pilot1234") and cannot change it. If you sign in with Google, your player name is the display name of your Google account, so other players will see it.
  • Matches against another player: while such a match runs, the server holds a match record that both phones read and write: for each of the two players the account ID, player name, avatar, trophies, deck with card levels and special ammo count, and the moves of the match (the cards picked, abilities used, round results). The other player sees your name, avatar, trophies and fleet during the match. The record is deleted when the match ends; one left behind by a lost connection is removed within minutes. There is no chat.
  • Google sign-in (optional): if you choose "Sign in with Google", Google confirms your account to Firebase Authentication, which then holds your Google account's display name, email address and profile picture address with your game account. The game itself uses only the display name; it never sees your password. Signing in turns your guest account into that account.
  • Cloud save (only with Google sign-in): a copy of your game progress is stored with your account, so it can be restored when you sign in on another phone or after reinstalling the game. Only you can read it. A guest's progress is kept only on the phone.
  • Transfer code (only if you create one): a copy of your game progress under a random 8-character code, so another phone can take it over. It works for 24 hours, is deleted when it is used, when you create a new code, or when you delete your online data.
  • Like any web service, Firebase processes your IP address to deliver the connection and protect the service from abuse. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS). We use it only to run the game's online features; we do not sell it and do not use it for advertising. Without a connection the game plays offline and nothing is sent.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads appear as optional videos you can choose to watch for in-game rewards, and occasionally between matches. They are provided by Google AdMob. When the app requests or shows an ad, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, your IP address and an approximate location derived from it, device and app information, interactions with ads, and diagnostic data. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud and to show ads based on your interests — unless you opt out or, where the law requires it, only with your consent. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. Club members and buyers of "No Ads" get no ads between matches.

Notifications (optional)

After a match the game asks once whether it may remind you when something is waiting for you: a free crate, a full special ammo stock, the daily gift, quest or season rewards that are about to expire, or that you have been away for some days. If you agree, Android asks for its notification permission. The reminders are scheduled by the game on your phone itself when you leave the game and cleared when you return; they are not sent from a server, and no information about them leaves your phone. You can turn them on or off at any time in the game (Settings → Notifications) or in Android's settings for the app.

Purchases (Google Play)

Gem packs, the starter pack, "No Ads", the season pass and the monthly Club subscription are bought through Google Play's own purchase screen. Google Play handles the payment; we never receive your payment details. The app receives the purchase record Google Play provides (product, state and a purchase token), checks it on the device and grants the item. You can cancel the Club at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Crates contain random items; their drop rates are shown in the game (tap the (i) on a crate).

Your choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and other places where the law requires it), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Google → Ads (on some devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You can change your avatar for free at any time in the game's profile. Player names cannot be typed in: a guest keeps the name the game gave, a Google account plays under its own name. To remove your name and profile from the server, use Settings → Delete my online data.

Deleting your data

  • Online data (profile, leaderboard entry, transfer code, cloud save and the game account, including a linked Google sign-in): in the game, Settings → Delete my online data. It is deleted from the server immediately, and online features stay off on that phone until you turn them back on. You can also ask by email (below).
  • Progress on the phone: Android Settings → Apps → Spaceships (the game's name on the phone) → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Advertising data is held by Google; purchase records by Google Play under your Google account.

Permissions

The only permission the app asks you for is the notification permission (Android 13 and later), and only after you choose to allow reminders in the game. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location or files. Without asking, as Android allows, it uses the internet and network state permissions (ads, Google Play, the game server), vibration for haptic feedback, Google Play's billing permission, and the permissions Google's ad SDK declares: the advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and Android's ad services.

Children

Spaceship Battle Fleet contains mild cartoon science-fiction violence between spaceships. It is not directed at children under 13, and the ads in it are limited to content suitable for a general audience with parental guidance.

Third parties

The app is built with the Unity engine; Unity's own analytics, ads and purchasing services are switched off. The third-party services in the app are Google AdMob with Google's User Messaging Platform for consent, Google Play Billing, Google Play's in-app review, and Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) for the game server. The Firebase SDK is not included; the game talks to Firebase's web services directly. Reminders use Unity's notifications component, which works on the phone only. The fonts in the game (Archivo Black, Noto Sans) are used under the SIL Open Font License.
