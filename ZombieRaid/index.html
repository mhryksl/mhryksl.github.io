Privacy Policy — Zombie Raid

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Applies to com.mhr.zombieraid (Zombie Raid: Loot and Escape)

Zombie Raid is a single-player action game published by Mahir Yüksel. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does not do with information.

Short version

  • We do not collect any personal data ourselves. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics of our own and no server of ours. Your progress stays on your device.
  • The game shows ads, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google's ad software in the app collects some information from your device, described below.
  • The optional premium subscription is sold and paid through Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally so you can close it and come back. That save contains only:

  • your game progress: stages reached, heroes, weapons, upgrades, items and in-game currencies;
  • timers for in-game rewards, and until when your premium subscription was last confirmed by Google Play;
  • how often the game has offered Google Play's rating card;
  • your sound, vibration and language preferences.

This is written with Android's standard app-preferences storage, stays inside the app's own private storage area, and is never uploaded by us. It contains no name, no email address, no device identifier, and no location. If you use Android's backup, Android may include it in your own device backup.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads appear between some raids, and as optional videos you can choose to watch for in-game rewards. They are provided by Google AdMob. When the app requests or shows an ad, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language;
  • interactions with ads (for example, which ads were shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the SDK.

Google uses this to deliver ads, to measure them, to prevent fraud and to show ads based on your interests — unless you opt out (see below) or, where the law requires it, only with your consent. The information goes to Google, not to us; we see only aggregated reports. How Google uses it is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy. Premium subscribers see no ads, and the ad software is not started for them.

Premium subscription (Google Play)

Premium is an optional monthly subscription bought through Google Play's own purchase screen. Google Play handles the payment and your payment details; we never receive them. To unlock premium, the app asks Google Play whether your Google account has an active subscription, and receives the purchase record Google Play provides (the product, its state and a purchase token), which the app checks on the device. You can cancel at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Google's handling of purchases is described in Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Ratings

After some successful raids the game may ask Google Play to show its rating card. The card, and any rating or review you give in it, belongs to Google Play; the game is not told whether or how you rated it.

Your choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and other places where the law requires it), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time: Settings → Privacy → Options in the game.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads: Android Settings → Google → Ads (on some devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Without a network connection the game still plays, and no ad data is sent.

Deleting your data

  • Your progress — Android Settings → Apps → Zombie Raid → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. Nothing of it is held anywhere else by us.
  • Advertising data is held by Google. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID (see above) breaks its link to your device; Google's own tools are described in its Privacy Policy.
  • Purchase records are held by Google Play, under your Google account.

Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files. It uses the network permission to fetch ads and to talk to Google Play, vibration for haptic feedback, Google Play's billing permission for the subscription, and Google's ad SDK declares the advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID).

Children

Zombie Raid contains cartoon fantasy violence against zombies. It is not directed at children under 13, and the ads in it are limited to content suitable for a general audience with parental guidance.

Third parties

The app is built with the Unity engine; Unity's own analytics, ads, purchasing and reporting services are switched off in the build. The third-party services in the app are Google AdMob with Google's User Messaging Platform for consent, Google Play Billing for the subscription, and Google Play's in-app review. There is no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting service of ours.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes what is collected or shared, this page will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top will change.
